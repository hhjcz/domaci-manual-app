# Main water shut-off

The main valve is in the technical room, on the wall behind the door, about
30 cm above the floor.

1. Turn the red lever a quarter turn clockwise until it is across the pipe.
2. Open the lowest tap in the house to release pressure.
3. Note the water meter reading before calling anyone.

## Individual shut-offs

- Bathroom — under the sink, two chrome valves.
- Kitchen — inside the sink cabinet.
- Garden — in the shaft by the gate, closed for winter.
