# Heat pump

The heat pump handles both heating and domestic hot water.

## Normal operation

| Setting        | Winter | Summer |
| -------------- | ------ | ------ |
| Heating curve  | 1.2    | off    |
| Hot water      | 48 °C  | 48 °C  |

## Restarting after a power cut

1. Wait five minutes so the compressor pressure equalises.
2. Turn the isolator switch next to the unit back on.
3. Check the indoor unit shows no error code.

!!! warning "Do not cycle repeatedly"
    Restarting more than twice in a row can lock the compressor out for
    two hours.

## Error codes

- `E5` — flow too low, check the filter in the technical room.
- `E9` — outdoor sensor fault, call service.
